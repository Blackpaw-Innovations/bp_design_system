Car Parts principle comparison

What must transfer—and what must not

Transfer the discipline

  • One shared shell and route owner.
  • Tokenized layout, typography and state.
  • Deterministic hierarchy and back behavior.
  • Stable overlays at desktop and mobile.
  • Real loading, empty, error and recovery states.
  • Measured core-task effort.

Do not copy the automotive skin

  • Dairy keeps its domain vocabulary and operational priorities.
  • Role homes may differ when evidence supports them.
  • Density may vary through a named archetype.
  • No Car Parts-specific colours, imagery or workflow semantics become platform rules.

Closure sequence

Repair in dependency order

  1. Production deliveryBuild immutable assets, remove Vite/HMR, apply headers, prove non-blank render.
  2. Foundation adoptionPin the design system, self-host Urbanist and replace private token systems.
  3. Shell reconciliationAdopt the route, navigation, modal and page-archetype contracts.
  4. Authenticated auditExercise every role and route at all required viewports, including six page states.
  5. CertificationAttach evidence to one commit/environment and pass DS-01–DS-13 with no expired exception.
Scope and honesty

Evidence was captured from the public deployment on 7 September 2026. The audit does not claim that unrendered authenticated pages are visually defective; it records them as unverified. A second round begins only after the production-delivery blocker is removed and test-role access is available.
