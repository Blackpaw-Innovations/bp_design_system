01 · Direction

The proposal in one thought

Blackpaw needs one coherent system that guides products, documents, language and experiences without forcing every application into the same mood.

Blackpaw normally feels bright, clear and intelligent. Light blue carries the work; purple creates focus.

Dark navy brings authority. Coral brings human energy. White creates room to understand. The signature gradient is reserved for moments that deserve ceremony.

05 · Typography

Urbanist is the application law

The repository's licensed Urbanist variable files are loaded by this artefact. The remaining typography decisions concern documents and signature moments—not exceptions to the app rule.

Locked

Urbanist

Simple should feel effortless.

Every Blackpaw and Hakiqa application. UI, controls, navigation, data and product communication. No product-level font substitutions.

Proposed document default

Urbanist first

One family, fewer inconsistencies.

Use Urbanist for digital and generated documents by default. Test Google Sans only as an office-document alternative where embedding and licensing are confirmed.

Reserved

Gotham

Signature, not system.

Retain for approved logo artwork, major brand declarations and selected campaign headlines. Do not require it for routine documents or applications.

07 · Document system

Consistency below the colour
