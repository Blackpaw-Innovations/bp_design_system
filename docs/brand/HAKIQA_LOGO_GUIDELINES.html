Hakiqa logo usage

A practical guide to the approved symbol, wordmarks, lockups, icons, print assets and motion files.

The idea

Two opening laptops form a four-panel mark: teal and orange screens above navy and red bases. The central negative space is part of the identity. Use the supplied files so the panel spacing, hinge relationship and smooth corners stay intact.

Approved symbol

Keep all four panels at every size.

Approved lettering

Capitalised Hakiqa is the default. The approved brush script is the expressive alternative.

Construction and space

The symbol is a fixed composition. Do not reposition or redraw its four paths. Use the approved horizontal and stacked lockups as complete files. Give them clear breathing room from nearby text, borders and crop edges.

The square app icon exports have 12% visible padding per side; the social avatar has 18%. Those values describe the approved exports and are not a new universal rule.

Size and colour

The supplied favicon supports 16 px and 32 px with the full four-panel mark. Fine gaps can soften naturally at 16 px; do not omit panels or manually close spaces. For receipts use the supplied 384 px one-bit black file. Use a legibility proof at final print or display size for larger lockups.

Use the supplied full, dark-field, gloss, orange, navy, white, black and orange/navy variants for their intended backgrounds.

Keep it intact

Do not crop away the bottom panels, redraw the gaps, or round corners independently.
Do not stretch, recolour, trace or replace the approved symbol with the Haki character illustration.
Do not invent a lowercase-only or all-caps wordmark.

Motion

Play the opening animation once. Use the quiet loop only for a real loading state. Both Lottie files preserve the approved four-panel paths and have static PNG fallbacks; the opening also has an MP4. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
