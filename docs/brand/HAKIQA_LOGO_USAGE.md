# Hakiqa logo usage / Batch 7 review

8 October 2026 · Application guide for the approved Hakiqa identity

## Source artwork

Use the approved four-panel symbol in `src/assets/brand/hakiqa/logo/approved/`. Its teal and orange screens sit over the navy and red bases. Keep all four panels, their gaps, corner curves and proportions. Choose an approved colour treatment by background; do not redraw, stretch, or shift individual paths in a static logo.

Use only the approved capitalised **Hakiqa** and brush-script **Hakiqa** wordmarks. The capitalised wordmark is the default for product and interface use. The script is for expressive moments where its small strokes remain legible. Do not substitute lowercase-only or all-capitals lettering.

## Layout and space

Choose a horizontal or stacked lockup from the approved Batch 3 and 4 files. Use its existing proportions as supplied. Keep nearby text, borders, photography and crop edges clear of the symbol and wordmark. In app icons, retain the approved Batch 5 visible padding: 12% on each side for square icons; the social avatar uses 18% visible padding. These measurements describe those exports and should not be applied to unrelated formats as a new brand-wide rule.

The full four-panel mark is the favicon even at 16 px. Fine seams may merge naturally through antialiasing at that size. Do not delete panels, close gaps or redraw a special small symbol. Use the supplied native 16 px and 32 px PNGs and the SVG favicon.

## Colour and production

Use the supplied full, full-dark, gloss, orange, navy, white, black or orange/navy variants. Use the matched file for light or dark fields rather than recolouring one ad hoc. The receipt logo is a 384 px, one-bit black PNG on white for thermal printing. Print and sticker vendors should proof physical size and cut line from the final vector artwork before production.

The Duka and Connect product wordmarks use the approved capitalised Hakiqa artwork with lighter Urbanist product names. The Pay wordmark remains unmade until that product is confirmed live.

## Stickers in this batch

The capitalised and script stickers use approved wordmark artwork with a white rounded backing. The **Ipo kwa duka** sticker uses the full-dark approved mark on navy with outlined Urbanist lettering. These three sticker compositions are Batch 7 review proposals; the mark and wordmark sources are already approved.

## Motion in this batch

- **Assembly:** four intact panels settle into the approved symbol once, over two seconds.
- **Quiet loading loop:** panels move only a few pixels and return over 2.4 seconds; all four remain visible.
- **Lockup reveal:** the approved horizontal capitalised lockup is uncovered once over two seconds.

Each animated SVG includes a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback. Use the supplied static PNG poster when motion is not suitable. The MP4 previews have white backgrounds. Avoid continuous motion outside a real loading or progress state, and never make a task wait for a logo animation.
