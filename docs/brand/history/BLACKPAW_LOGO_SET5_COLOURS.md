# Blackpaw logo set v2 (8 Oct 2026)

Approved on the logo board: **1a midnight** and **1b ink navy** as the dark backgrounds, plus the **rebuilt gradient (2a)**. Pure black has been dropped.

## Colours
- Midnight #140F33 with a violet glow (#7A2FA8 at 45%) top-left and a plum glow (#4B1E55 at 70%) bottom-right. For digital and social.
- Ink navy #0A0A3A, flat. For print, documents and anywhere the glow is too much.
- Gradient: violet base (#3B1C7A → #7A2FA8 → #4A23B8) with coral #FF6461 top-right, indigo #2A1AD0 bottom-right and ink #0A0A3A bottom-left.
- On dark backgrounds the mark's violet and blue half is brightened (`-color-dark`), and the text is warm white #FBF7F2.
