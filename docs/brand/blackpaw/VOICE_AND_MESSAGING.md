# Voice and Messaging

## Message hierarchy

These statements have different jobs. Do not rotate them as interchangeable
taglines.

| Level | Approved language | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Customer proposition | **We make complex businesses easier to run.** | Website hero, company introduction, proposal opening |
| Mission | **Build Africa's future on honest technology.** | Strategy, recruitment, culture, founder communication |
| Company description | **An African business-systems and technology architecture company.** | Boilerplate, profiles, press, metadata |
| Brand promise | **It just works.** | Experience principle, campaign support, product narrative |
| Reputation aim | **You call Blackpaw when you want it done right.** | Internal standard, case-study close, selective campaign copy |
| Corporate tagline | **Inspiring the Extraordinary** | Approved logo lockup, corporate signature, brand campaigns |
| Design doctrine | **Low effort in front. High rigour behind.** | Product, service-design and engineering guidance |

## Boilerplate

### 25 words

Blackpaw designs, builds and operates connected business systems that make
complex organisations simpler, clearer and more reliable.

### 50 words

Blackpaw Innovations is an African business-systems and technology architecture
company. We understand how organisations work, then design, build and operate
connected systems across finance, operations, customers and teams. Our work
combines operational insight, Odoo, specialised software, automation and ongoing
support.

### Extended

Blackpaw Innovations helps organisations replace fragmented tools, manual
hand-offs and opaque reporting with connected systems they can depend on. We
begin with operational truth, then combine business-systems architecture, Odoo,
specialised modules, integrations, product design and managed support. Founded
in Nairobi, we build for African realities to a standard credible anywhere in
the world.

## Voice

Blackpaw sounds:

- **clear** — familiar words, concrete meaning and a visible next action;
- **assured** — decisive where evidence exists, candid where it does not;
- **intelligent** — insightful without performing cleverness;
- **human** — warm and respectful without becoming flippant;
- **specific** — outcomes and evidence instead of vague superlatives; and
- **responsible** — no absolute reliability, security or transformation claims.

## Writing rules

1. Lead with the business reality, not the technology stack.
2. Explain the outcome before the mechanism.
3. Prefer a concrete verb: reconcile, connect, approve, recover, see.
4. Use short headlines and natural sentence case.
5. Address the reader as “you” only when the context is genuinely theirs.
6. Replace “seamless”, “revolutionary” and “world-class” with evidence.
7. Do not promise percentages unless measurement and scope are stated.
8. Do not call every organisation complex, extraordinary or unique.
9. Distinguish what exists now from what Blackpaw is building toward.
10. Treat Swahili and local language as real communication, not decorative slang.

## Copy transformations

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| We provide cutting-edge digital transformation solutions. | We connect finance, stock, sales and operations so your team can work from the same truth. |
| Automate 80% of your manual workflows. | We identify repeatable work, automate it safely and measure the time returned to your team. |
| A seamless system where every component works in perfect harmony. | One connected system, with clear ownership when an integration fails. |
| Generic software is for generic companies. | Your system should reflect how your business actually works. |
| We don't stop until we've found the best solution. | We validate the solution against the operation, its risks and the people who use it. |
| Transforming Africa through disruptive entrepreneurship. | Building Africa's future on honest technology. |

## Website narrative

The primary website should follow this order:

1. **Outcome:** We make complex businesses easier to run.
2. **Problem:** fragmented tools, repeated work and unclear performance.
3. **Method:** understand, architect, build, operate and improve.
4. **Capability:** business systems, Odoo, integrations, products and support.
5. **Proof:** named outcomes, client evidence and operational measures.
6. **Fit:** who Blackpaw serves and when it is not the right partner.
7. **Action:** discuss an operational problem or request an assessment.

Avoid presenting every industry and technology as an equal speciality. Use case
studies to prove vertical competence.

## Sales language

Sell the movement from one operating condition to another:

> Today, stock, finance and customer information are reconciled manually. We
> will establish one governed flow, make exceptions visible and give management
> a dependable operating view.

Every proposal should name:

- the current operational cost or risk;
- the intended business outcome;
- scope and exclusions;
- how success will be evidenced;
- client responsibilities; and
- what happens after launch.

## Failure communication

Use this order:

1. What happened.
2. Who or what is affected.
3. What remains safe.
4. What Blackpaw is doing now.
5. What the customer should do, if anything.
6. When the next update will arrive.
7. What will change after recovery.

Never hide an incident behind jargon or use “It just works” to deny normal
technical risk.

## Hakiqa voice

Hakiqa is simpler, warmer and closer to everyday work than corporate Blackpaw.
It may use accessible Kenyan English or carefully reviewed Swahili where that
improves understanding. It must still be precise about money, data, permissions
and failure.

Blackpaw says: **“Operational truth, designed into the system.”**  
Hakiqa says: **“Your biashara, clear and under control.”**
