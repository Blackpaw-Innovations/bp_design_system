StatusChip

--bp-success --bp-warning --bp-danger

A compact status pill. Manrope label-sm, optional pulsing dot for live states. Tones come straight from the signal tokens so meaning is consistent everywhere.

Six tonesaccent · success · warning · danger · info · neutral
Live Paid Pending Overdue Info Draft
No dot variant Pulsing = realtime 3 overdue
Do
Reserve the pulsing dot for genuinely live/streaming states. Match tone to meaning — danger is only for danger.
Don't
Invent new colors for chips or use accent for everything. Six tones is the whole vocabulary.
