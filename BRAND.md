# Blackpaw brand quick guide

Status: Canonical · Version 1.0 · Approved 2026

Blackpaw Innovations is **an African business-systems and technology architecture company**. We make complex businesses easier to run. Our mission is to **build Africa's future on honest technology**.

## The hierarchy

- Customer proposition: **We make complex businesses easier to run.**
- Mission: **Build Africa's future on honest technology.**
- Promise: **It just works.**
- Reputation aim: **You call Blackpaw when you want it done right.**
- Corporate tagline: **Inspiring the Extraordinary.**
- Design doctrine: **Low effort in front. High rigour behind.**

These lines have different jobs. Do not collapse them into one slogan or use them all on one surface.

## Visual decisions

- All apps use **Urbanist**.
- The default document expression is **Clear**: Light Blue `#EBEAFA` and White, with Purple `#451D6A` for focus.
- Other approved Blackpaw modes are Authority (Purple + Dark), Human (Coral + White), Precision (Blue + White), and Signature (controlled multi-colour moments).
- Hakiqa remains distinct: Cyan, White, Orange, and Navy.
- Gotham is a legacy/signature reference, not an app font. Google Sans is not the default and must not appear in apps.

Do not implement these hex values directly. Use the semantic tokens in `src/tokens/index.css` or the mappings in `tailwind.preset.js`.
